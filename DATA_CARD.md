## Windows and features (Phase 3) — FINAL for the 442-case sample (2026-10-24)

- Generated by `scripts/03_build_windows.py` (`src/drift/features.py`) over the
  442 final-eligible cases from `data/interim/cohort.parquet`.
- Prediction times: every 1 minute, starting once each case's patient baseline
  closes (exclusion window + baseline window after anesthesia start, clamped to
  not precede the operation/recording start — see note below), through `opend`.
  **4/442 cases (0.9%) excluded**: no usable MAP/SBP/DBP samples anywhere in
  their baseline window (artifact-rejected throughout, e.g. arterial-line
  calibration right after placement) — **438 cases contribute windows.**
- **Total windows: 66,871.**
- Exclusion rule (PLAN.md 7.2, MAP<65 in the previous 5 min) drops the row
  entirely rather than keeping it flagged.
- **Positive rate by horizon and cohort** (label = event starts within the
  next H minutes; "unknown" = case recording ends before the horizon closes,
  i.e. right-censored, not counted as negative):

  | H (min) | Overall | Cohort B | non-Cohort-B | % unknown/censored |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 5  | 3.90% | 5.37% | 2.72%  | 2.32%  |
  | 10 | 7.54% | 10.18% | 5.40% | 5.14%  |
  | 15 (primary) | 10.77% | 14.36% | 7.81% | 7.94%  |
  | 30 | 18.48% | 23.65% | 14.03% | 16.14% |

  Cohort B's positive rate is consistently ~1.7-2x non-Cohort-B's at every
  horizon — expected given Cohort B is selected for high blood loss/transfusion,
  and matches the event-count enrichment already noted under Cohorts (Phase 2).
- **Data-quirk note, documented rather than silently patched:** `anestart`
  (anesthesia start, seconds from casestart) is frequently negative — median
  -660s across the 442 eligible cases — because the arterial line (and so our
  four required numeric tracks) is commonly placed, and the recording starts,
  *after* anesthesia induction. The naive baseline window
  (`anestart + exclusion_window`) can then start before any data exists at all.
  Fixed by clamping the baseline-window start to `max(anestart + exclusion_window,
  opstart, 0)`, mirroring `cohort.usable_monitored_minutes()`'s own bounds. Before
  this fix, 194/442 cases (44%) were wrongly excluded for "no baseline" — after
  it, only the 4 genuinely-artifact-only cases above are excluded.
- Design choices PLAN.md leaves unspecified (documented in `features.py`'s
  module docstring, not guessed dataset facts): MAP std/min and the
  missing-fraction feature use the 15-minute window (the longest slope window)
  rather than a new undocumented window length; drift-from-baseline is computed
  for HR, MAP, PP and shock index.

## Known caveats

- EBL and transfusion totals are end-of-case, with no timing — they only select
  which cases go in Cohort B; they are never model input features.
- This is surgical (intraoperative) data, not prehospital trauma data. The label
  is low blood pressure, not hemorrhage specifically.
- Single hospital, single country, 2016–2017. Not validated for generalization.
