MAP Mean arterial pressure: the average pressure pushing blood through the body over one heartbeat. The standard monitor alarms when it stays below the red line on the chart.—
Pulse pressure The gap between the top (systolic) and bottom (diastolic) blood-pressure numbers. In bleeding it often narrows early, while MAP still looks normal.—
Shock index Heart rate divided by systolic blood pressure. It rises when the heart speeds up to make up for falling pressure.—
Risk of low pressure in next 15 min The trained model's estimate that MAP drops below the red line soon. On this real surgical data it is calculated from MAP alone.
